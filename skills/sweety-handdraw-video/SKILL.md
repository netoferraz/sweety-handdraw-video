# Themed Hand-Drawn Video

Deliver a playable final video, cover, subtitles, voiceover script, and verification records. Defaults: Simplified Chinese, 1920×1080, 30fps; the user's language, dimensions, art style, voice, and reuse requirements take priority. Runtime follows the content and the actual voiceover; page count, layout, and per-page duration are not fixed.

## Getting Started

First read [Dependency Routing](references/dependencies.md), then read the built-in guides as needed. They are this skill's production references, require no separate installation, and do not take over the task on their own. The model designs the substantive content by reasoning; scripts only execute finalized assets and timelines.

First check whether a theme, story, full text, attachments, or an explicit source already exists. Only when all of these are missing, pause and ask for a theme or story; all other production preferences are optional and never constitute a blocker requiring re-approval.

1. **Explain the causality.** Determine the audience, the problem to solve, the mechanism, necessary counterexamples, and actions. For article adaptations, read the full text first and preserve qualifications, attribution, and sources; verify time-sensitive or disputed facts against primary sources. By default evaluate whether "common everyday mistake → concept → mechanism → action → closing" applies; user-specified approaches take priority, and when it does not apply, plan with the built-in director methods. Record the judgment in `design.json`; see [Story & Storyboard](references/story-design.md).
2. **Offer choices at the start.** Per [Optional Production Settings](references/production-options.md), have the model prepare a default plan from the content, then present art style, arm/pen-only, voice, pen-barrel advertising, floating watermark, and subtitles in order. Already-decided options carry over directly. Wait at most 60 seconds per item; on timeout save the default plan and move to the next item — never end the conversation waiting for another round; when the user has explicitly delegated full automation, all steps may be skipped. Show real previews for art style plus three complete options at the end; record the choice in `style/selection.json`, and the model resolves it into `style/resolved.json`; in-catalog matching, independent inference, and custom descriptions must not be mixed. Advertising and watermarks default to off; never invent brands or attributions.
3. **Full voiceover first.** Complete the full narration script first, then generate the entire narration in one pass with the selected voice — via the available local VoiceStudio, or an already-installed and explicitly configured local VoxCPM2; a user-approved complete recording may also be reused. Preserve natural breaths, semantic pauses, and sentence endings; do not generate sliced per-frame or per-subtitle clips, and do not insert fixed silence at image transitions. By default deliver narration only — never automatically synthesize or mix in background music; required sound effects must state their narrative purpose and bind to specific sentences. After fully checking text, sentence endings, abnormally long pauses, and continuous listening feel, lock the narration master, then map word-/sentence-level timestamps onto visuals and subtitles. Sound sets the rhythm; images adapt to sound. Voice selection defaults to the vetted primary voice (preferred) in the machine's personal voice catalog, with the user's explicit choice for this session taking priority. The voice catalog keeps only six entries: lifelike male, lifelike female, sweet senior, Iapetus, intellectual female lecturer, story narrator. Lifelike male reuses the original reference from "The Snake That Died of Anger"; lifelike female reuses the extracted human voice from the current original of "How Happiness Imprisons You"; both provide excerpt auditions from the finished films, while the other four provide same-text auditions. Personal and task directories may only supplement references for these six entries — never add back old names or other voices into the selector; do not clone a voice from a synthesized audition to impersonate an original-film voice. For original-film voices, first check the same-text and short body-text samples, then verify the complete narration; no background music does not mean the narration is noise-free. See [Sound & Dependencies](references/local-runtime.md) for import and operation. Never auto-purchase services, download models, change global engines, or deliver a silent film.
4. **Design drawable art.** Every generated image, candidate, edit, cover, and new hand asset must first apply the built-in art direction; in-catalog styles keep their complete recipes; independent-inference and custom styles use only their own style specifications. First write `drawing-plan.json` and run the pre-generation check, then write the brief and prompts; for each illustration list subject, parts, order, independent strokes, and the outline plan. No image generation may be called without an executable plan. List the corresponding source text and visible narrative facts for each image in narrative_requirements; bites, breaks, contact, losing or gaining an object must be genuinely visible — never substitute titles or subtitles alone. Fill in narrative_checks per item while viewing the images; stop downstream builds on failure. Build a representative frame first, verify subject, contact, composition, and part separability, then expand to the rest of the film's assets. New themes are redesigned; revisions of this film or explicitly requested reuse keep approved images and audio. Cover hero visuals may be AI-generated, with accurate text composited in editable layers. All generated images undergo the built-in privacy cleanup, removing simulated-camera fields, then checking metadata and source attributes.
5. **Split parts from the actual image.** Typically in order: head, neck, torso, frame-left arm, right arm, lower body, and props; explain exceptions for unusual compositions. The model annotates semantic regions by viewing the image, handling occlusion — never crudely slicing a figure into rectangles, and never marking a whole person or whole scene as a single part. Record the occlusion reason for invisible parts; do not invent outlines. Complete all outlines for the whole image first, then fill colors in the same order. Strokes are independent with pen lifts between them; even adjacent strokes sharing an endpoint need a lift gap, and broken lines must not be joined; color appears only where the brush has passed. Each glyph is isolated — later characters must not show before earlier ones finish; keep margins at true glyph boundaries, checking descenders, punctuation, and Latin descenders.
6. **Layout and representative-segment check.** First fix the subtitle form per [Subtitle & Ending Design](references/subtitle-design.md): upright, legible, concise, and unobtrusive; choose no-background or light-plate by the visuals, keeping any user-approved design; fonts are independent of body handwriting, shown as short spoken phrases with the closing after the final sentence checked. By default hold the complete frame — no long empty holds; never fade out or cut audio before the final character ends; master tail protection and finished-film silence caps follow the [Sound & Ending Hard Constraints](references/audio-and-ending.md). User-specified closing lines must join the complete script before synthesis. Then write tasks per the [Continuous Narration Convention](references/continuous-narration.md) and [Runtime Format](references/runtime-contract.md). Frames accumulate around complete meanings, with complexity suited to the real speech duration. Fixed 1-second cover: static for the first 0.5s, fading out over the next 0.5s, then body and narration begin. First render a representative segment covering text, illustration, coloring, subtitles, and transitions; check nib, arm, occlusion, reading holds, and audio-visual correspondence before rendering the full film.
7. **Verify, then deliver.** Check final files per [Quality Gates](references/quality-gates.md). Record engineering, visual, motion, content, and audio evidence separately; on failure fix the corresponding layer and re-render. Without actually listening, state clearly that subjective listening is unverified; never present decoding, ASR, file existence, or rule loading as guarantees of perceived quality. `finalize` only accepts verification records matching the current video. On delivery, state explicitly: "No background music added by default; you may mix your own or pick music on the publishing platform."

Missing locked full narration and matching timestamps, unresolved default/selected style, missing pre-generation check, mismatched parts, whole-person single block, missing outlines, lines bridging across blank gaps, or expired verification records cause production build/render to reject the input; the model fixes these and continues. Unselected preferences never trigger this gate. Scripts may only check structure, hashes, pixels, and paths; they cannot replace the model viewing images to judge head/neck, occlusion, stroke intent, and style quality.

## All Image Generation Must Pass Art Direction First

Before every image-generation or image-edit tool call in this project, the full `dependencies/sweety-image-art-direction/GUIDE.md` must be read and applied; this is the user's explicit authorization for this project, overriding that guide's standalone "manual-trigger-only" rule. Covers, body art, characters, props, new hand assets, candidates, variants, partial edits, and rework all apply; skipping with "just trying", "reusing the style", or "only a small change" is forbidden.

First write the image brief and the complete prompt actually used this time, save them to the task directory; register `brief_file` and `prompt_file` in the art record, run `python scripts/art_direction.py bind-evidence <record path>` to bind content hashes, then call the image tool. Generating first and backfilling guidance records is forbidden. Changed prompts or briefs require re-running guidance, re-binding, and re-generating; the same finished image cannot pose as a new generation by updating records.

After generation, actually view the image and record the guidance version, cleaned image hash, visual verdict, and evidence. Missing records, empty brief/prompt, hash mismatch, failed visual review, or replaced images all halt build, render, and delivery. `bind-evidence` only binds existing text — it never fills in `pass` for the model, nor proves image quality. Reusing byte-identical approved assets may carry over the original record; once edited, a new round of evidence is required.

## Subtitle Uniqueness Is a Hard Delivery Gate

The same narration sentence may show only one subtitle at any moment. No two subtitle layers may overlap, whether from baked-in frame subtitles, subtitle events, embedded MP4 subtitle tracks, page `<track>` elements, or player auto-loaded subtitle files. Default: white text, thin black stroke, no plate; the uniqueness gate still applies when the user picks another form.

Re-synthesize only from subtitle-free masters plus an independent subtitle layer; never stack a second layer onto a finished film that already has hard subtitles. Build and render checks require cues to correspond one-to-one with subtitle events, with consistent roles and no time overlap; delivery checks require no extra subtitle tracks in the MP4 and no SRT/ASS/VTT subtitle files in the same directory. Edited subtitles go only in `outputs/editing/`; preview pages get no subtitle tracks. Finally, check long sentences, sentence transitions, and the ending in actual decoded frames; any double subtitles mean FAIL — fix before delivery. Auto-translate/live subtitles enabled proactively by third-party players must be switched off separately; an unreproduced display source must not be treated as identified.

## Hands and Advertising

The six arms are `navy` dark-blue-sleeve marker, `diamond-woman` fair skin with crushed-diamond long nails, `child-pencil` children's colored pencil, `waner-brush` Shangguan-Wan'er-inspired brush, `teacher-pen` white-shirt neutral pen, `cardigan-fountain` knit-sleeve fountain pen; plus `pen-only` brush-only with no arm. The model picks the default by content, anchored at `assets/hands/catalog.json`. Calibrate arms by nib-to-wrist scale and keep cut edges off-frame; calibrate pen-only by the full pen body without applying forearm rules.

`hand.pen_text` adds advertising on the pen barrel only, max 16 characters, default empty; keep user-provided text, checking contrast and legibility at normal size. When too long to read, prefer expanding the usable barrel layout or simplifying the design over silently altering the ad name. Custom skins, nails, pens, and sleeves follow the same art, privacy, and anchor checks. Crushed diamonds are not replaced with large gems; clear skin is not traded for blur.

`watermark.text` uses the user's original text, may wrap lines, default empty; generates a low-opacity, slowly drifting text watermark that stays in-frame and clear of subtitles. Parameters and checks: see [Optional Production Settings](references/production-options.md).

## Operation & Boundaries

`scripts/handdraw_video.py` provides `doctor / build / render / verify / finalize`. Read the runtime format before calling; task data goes in a standalone directory, never written into the install directory. `doctor` distinguishes new-narration generation services from offline synthesis conditions for existing audio. When required materials or capabilities are missing, report the specific blocker.

Quality first. Engineering gates verify execution order and completeness; the model still reviews semantic segmentation and design. Chinese characters are glyph-approximate tracing, with no guarantee of calligraphic stroke order; naturalness of real handwriting, emotion, and learning effectiveness are evaluated separately. No promise that any theme can be generated stably in one pass. No extra video publishing, service provisioning, or global model reconfiguration.

Public hands, voice presets, and preview images use [Hosted Assets](references/hosted-assets.md): download and verify on first use, then reuse the cache. Original-film recordings and the user's personal voice library stay local and never enter public hosting.
